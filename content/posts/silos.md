## What it is

A farm silo is a structure used to store feed or grain safely.

Common types include:

- **Tower silos** (tall vertical structures)
- **Bunker silos** (long concrete pits, often covered)
- **Bag silos** (large sealed plastic bags)

Many silos are used to store **silage**, which is fermented feed (often chopped corn or grass) for cattle.

## Why

Farmers use silos to preserve feed quality and reduce waste.

Silos help by:

- protecting feed from weather
- reducing spoilage and contamination
- storing large amounts in one place
- making feeding more consistent across seasons

This is especially important in winter or dry periods when fresh forage is limited.

## How does it work

For silage, the process is usually:

1. Harvest forage and chop it into small pieces.
2. Pack it tightly into a silo to remove oxygen.
3. Seal it so low-oxygen fermentation can happen.
4. Natural bacteria produce acids that preserve the feed.
5. Open and remove feed gradually as needed.

Good packing and sealing are critical. Too much air can cause mold, heat damage, and nutrient loss.
